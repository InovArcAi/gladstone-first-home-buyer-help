Fine print

Three things buyers here get caught by

Boost to Buy is lodged somewhere else

Unity Bank has been approved as the lender for Boost to Buy, and applications go there. Weighing the program against the alternatives is broker work and it gets covered in an opening conversation, but the lodgement belongs to Unity Bank.

The grant covers new homes, nothing else

A First Home Owner Grant of $30,000 attaches only where the home is new and under $750,000. Established stock does not qualify at all. Buy established and what you are working with is the duty concession, along with the 5% Deposit Scheme inside its own cap.

A residency test applies from 1 August 2026

From 1 August 2026, a home concession on Queensland transfer duty can only be claimed by someone holding Australian citizenship or permanent residency, or counted as a specified foreign retiree. Sign the contract before that day and it does not apply to you. Sign after it and it does.

Questions

What buyers here ask about the programs

What deposit do I actually need?
Lender and program together decide it. Through the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme, an eligible buyer gets in on 5% and carries no Lenders Mortgage Insurance, as long as the price stays under a Gladstone cap of $700,000. Outside that, Lenders Mortgage Insurance normally appears below 20%, and every lender sets minimums of its own.
Do I qualify for the Queensland grants?
One program at a time is how that gets decided. Income caps came off the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme back on 1 October 2025. Duty concessions reach a first home sitting inside their thresholds. On a new build below $750,000, an eligible buyer can claim the First Home Owner Grant. The table above links every one of those eligibility pages.
Can I use more than one at once?
Some pair up, some do not. One eligible purchase can carry both a duty concession and the First Home Owner Grant. Claim on vacant land, though, and the new home concession is closed off on that land afterwards. Unity Bank handles Boost to Buy direct, no broker involved. What is open to you follows from the contract date and the property itself.
What if interest rates move again?
Nobody can say where they head next. When it met on 11 August 2026, the Reserve Bank of Australia left the cash rate where it was, 4.35%. Every grant and concession on this page is written into legislation instead, so a rate move cannot strip them out. The part that does move is borrowing capacity, tested as it is against a buffer sitting over the real rate.
